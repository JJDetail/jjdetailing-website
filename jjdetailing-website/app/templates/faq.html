Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

Services

What's the difference between your detail packages?

Express Detail ($59.99): Basic exterior wash, interior vacuum, tire shine, and window cleaning. Perfect for regular maintenance. Takes about 1 hour.

Standard Detail ($149.99): Includes everything in Express plus clay bar treatment, wax application, interior deep clean, and leather/fabric conditioning. Takes about 3 hours.

Premium Detail ($299.99): Our most comprehensive service including paint correction, ceramic coating application, engine bay cleaning, and complete interior restoration. Takes 6+ hours.

How long does each service take?

Service times vary based on vehicle size and condition:

  • Express Detail: 45-60 minutes
  • Standard Detail: 2-3 hours
  • Premium Detail: 6-8 hours
  • Paint Correction: 4-6 hours
  • Ceramic Coating: 6-8 hours (may require overnight curing)
Do you offer mobile detailing services?

Yes! We offer mobile detailing services within a 15-mile radius of our location. We bring all necessary equipment and supplies to your home or office. Additional travel fees may apply for locations beyond 15 miles.

Booking & Scheduling

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 2-3 days in advance, especially for weekend appointments. During peak seasons (spring and summer), booking a week ahead is advisable. Same-day service may be available for Express Details, subject to availability.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

We understand plans change. Please give us at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a fee of 50% of the service price.

What are your business hours?

We're open:

  • Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Saturday: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
  • Sunday: Closed

Mobile service appointments may be available outside regular hours by special arrangement.

Pricing & Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept multiple payment methods for your convenience:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
  • Digital payments (Venmo, Zelle, Apple Pay, Google Pay)
  • Personal checks (with valid ID)

Payment is due upon completion of service.

Do you offer any discounts or packages?

Yes! We offer several ways to save:

  • First-time customer: 15% off any service
  • Monthly maintenance plans: Save 20% with regular service
  • Multi-vehicle discount: 10% off when detailing 2+ vehicles
  • Referral program: Get $25 off your next service for each referral

General Questions

What should I do to prepare for my appointment?

To ensure the best results:

  • Remove all personal items from the vehicle
  • Ensure we have access to water and electricity (for mobile service)
  • Let us know about any specific concerns or problem areas
  • Have at least 1/4 tank of gas (for moving the vehicle if needed)
Are your products safe for my vehicle?

Absolutely! We use only professional-grade, pH-balanced products that are safe for all vehicle surfaces. Our products are eco-friendly, biodegradable, and won't damage your vehicle's paint, interior materials, or protective coatings.

Do you offer any warranties or guarantees?

Yes! We stand behind our work:

  • 100% satisfaction guarantee on all services
  • Ceramic coatings come with a 2-year warranty
  • Paint correction results guaranteed for 6 months
  • If you're not happy, we'll make it right at no additional cost
