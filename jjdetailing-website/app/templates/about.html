About J&J Detailing

Your trusted partner in automotive care since 2015

Our Story

J&J Detailing was founded by two passionate car enthusiasts, John and James, who believed that every vehicle deserves to look its absolute best. What started as a weekend hobby quickly grew into a full-service detailing business serving hundreds of satisfied customers.

Our commitment to excellence, attention to detail, and use of premium products has made us the go-to choice for car owners who demand the best for their vehicles.

Today, we're proud to offer a comprehensive range of detailing services, from basic washes to advanced paint correction and ceramic coating applications.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional automotive detailing services that exceed customer expectations while maintaining the highest standards of quality and professionalism.

Our Vision

To be the premier choice for car detailing services in our community, known for our expertise, reliability, and commitment to customer satisfaction.

Our Values

Quality, integrity, innovation, and customer focus drive everything we do. We treat every vehicle as if it were our own.
